Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (demo)
Circular No. DEMO/2026/17 — Revised eligibility: PM-KISAN (illustrative)

Page 1
1. Purpose. This circular revises the eligibility conditions of the income-support scheme
   "PM-KISAN" for the purpose of demonstrating rule ingestion.

2. Eligibility. A farmer family is eligible if ALL of the following hold:
   (a) the family holds cultivable land recorded in the name of a family member;
   (b) the landholding is greater than zero hectares;
   (c) no member of the family paid income tax in the last assessment year;
   (d) the family has an active bank account (new in this circular).

Page 2
3. Exclusions. Tenant farmers and sharecroppers who do not hold land in their own name
   are not eligible under this scheme.

4. Examples.
   Example 1: Ramesh owns 1.2 hectares, has a bank account and nobody in his family pays income tax. He is eligible.
   Example 2: Sita cultivates 0.8 hectares as a tenant (land not in her name). She is not eligible.
   Example 3: Mohan owns 2 hectares but his son paid income tax last year. He is not eligible.
   Example 5: Arjun owns 1 hectare, pays no income tax, but has no bank account. He is not eligible.
   Example 4: Lata owns 0.5 hectares and has a bank account; we do not yet know whether anyone in her family pays income tax.
              She is likely eligible pending that check.

5. Documents. Aadhaar, land record, bank passbook.
